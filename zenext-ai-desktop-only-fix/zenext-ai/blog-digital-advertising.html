Digital Advertising: Reaching Customers in the Digital World

A clear look at how online advertising actually gets a business in front of the right people, across the platforms they already use.

The Basics

What is digital advertising?

Digital advertising means paying to show a message to people online, instead of relying only on them finding a business on their own. It covers everything from a video ad on YouTube to a search result at the top of Google.

📱

Social Media Ads

Ads shown inside apps people already scroll through daily.

🔎

Search Advertising

Ads shown when someone actively searches for something.

▶️

Video Advertising

Ads placed before, during or between video content.

🖥️

Display Advertising

Banner-style ads shown across websites and apps.

Behind every campaign are a few core ideas: targeting (who sees the ad), budget (how much is spent), creative (what the ad looks like), and the landing page people arrive at after clicking. Conversion tracking then shows what actually happened after the click, and optimization is the ongoing process of improving results based on that data.

Being Honest About It

Benefits and limitations

✅ Benefits

Reaches people across multiple digital touchpoints, not just one.

Every click and conversion can be measured and tracked.

Campaigns can be adjusted quickly based on real data.

⚠️ Limitations

A weak landing page can undo good targeting.

Different channels suit different kinds of businesses.

Management and creative costs are separate from ad spend.

Pricing Overview

Final Summary

Key takeaways

→Digital advertising spans social, search, video and display channels.
→Targeting, budget, creative and the landing page all work together.
→Tracking and optimization turn a campaign into an ongoing process.
→Costs vary by channel, and ad spend is separate from management.
