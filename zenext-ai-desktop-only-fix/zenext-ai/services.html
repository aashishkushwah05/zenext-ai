Services

The parts of your business that shouldn't need a person every time — handled automatically.

Zenext.ai builds three kinds of systems: ones that automate and converse, ones that give a business its digital presence, and ones that create and promote. Explore what each one solves below.

Seven solutions, one system of work

Pick a solution to see the problem it's built for, how it works, and what it looks like in practice.

AI Automation

Workflows that connect the tools you already use, so routine steps run on their own instead of waiting on someone to start them.

  • Manual data entry between tools
  • Repeated follow-ups and status updates
  • Tasks that only need a trigger, not a decision

AI Agents

Where automation runs a fixed set of steps, an agent works through a task with some judgment — checking, deciding, and moving to the next step on its own.

  • Multi-step requests that need context
  • Qualifying or sorting incoming work
  • Tasks that involve a small decision, not just a trigger

Voice & Calling Agents

AI voice agents that can answer, ask questions, and route calls in natural conversation — so incoming calls don't only get handled during office hours.

  • Calls that come in outside working hours
  • Repetitive questions handled by phone
  • Qualifying a caller before it reaches a person

WhatsApp Automation

Automated replies inside WhatsApp itself — where many customers already prefer to ask a question rather than call or fill a form.

  • Repeated enquiries at all hours
  • Booking or availability questions
  • Passing the right requests on to staff

Website Development

Websites built around what the business actually does — fast, responsive, and structured so they're easy to extend later.

  • No real digital presence yet
  • A site that doesn't work well on mobile
  • An online presence that needs to grow with the business

Creative & Growth

Content, video editing, and ad campaigns — built and read by what performs, so the creative work and the media spend support each other.

  • Content that needs planning, not just posting
  • Video that needs a real edit, not a rough cut
  • Ad spend without a clear read on performance

SEO & Google Ranking

Improve how your business appears in search, strengthen the pages that matter, and make it easier for potential customers to find you online.

  • Low visibility in relevant Google searches
  • Website pages that are not structured for search
  • Business information that is hard for customers to discover

Featured solution

WhatsApp automation for hotels & restaurants

A practical example: many enquiries a hotel or restaurant receives are the same few questions, asked at all hours. WhatsApp automation can pick up the first response, and hand off to staff when it actually needs a person.
